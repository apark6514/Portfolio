About

Hello! My name is Andrew Park and I am a Junior in Mechanical Engineering the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign planning to graduate in May 2027. I'm extremely passionate about electronics and mechatronics as well as combustion and propulsion systems and have extensive experience in both fields.

I am currently wrapping up my internship at Google on the Pixel Product Design Engineering team in Chicago, IL, working on novel shielding solutions for Pixel 2028. My team has been nothing but supportive, and I've learned a great deal in the areas of DFM for sheet metal and die cuts, EMI shielding, design of experiments, and more.

In the past, I've had the opportunity to work at many companies, including SpaceX, where I worked with fluids, testing, and system design and activation, Caterpillar Inc., where I developed a custom electrical test system to measure on-engine solenoid temperatures, and Argonne National Laboratory, where I developed a novel algorithm to characterize key components and attributes from sustainable fuel combustion experiments. Click here to learn more about my industry experience!

When it comes to projects, I've been lucky enough to have a variety of experiences that have helped me cultivate many skills in various disciplines. Last year, I led a 6-Axis Robotic Arm project with over 40 members as well as a Liquid Rocket Engine Analysis team that performed both thermal and structural analysis on the combustion chamber and nozzle of a liquid rocket engine. I've done many other projects as well, and you can view the full list here.

While I am very technically focused, I'm also a real human and greatly value my free time. I love music, exercise, and Jesus and have played piano for 15 years. I also play the violin and am slowly learning the guitar. I just finished studying abroad in Switzerland for the Fall 2025 semester and truly enjoyed travelling Europe and experiencing the beautiful nature and culture there!
